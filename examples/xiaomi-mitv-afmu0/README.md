# Xiaomi TV Box S 3rd gen (MiTV-AFMU0, twilight)

Google TV on Android 14 (SDK 34), Amlogic, 2 GB RAM. Tested with Luxury TV as Home.

- `luxury.txt`: a tested debloat list for this box (53 packages, by section). Start here.

Measured on this box with Luxury TV as Home, idle: about 1 GB of its 2 GB RAM free, Luxury TV about 85 MB.
Google Play services (`com.google.android.gms`) stays the largest process at about 115 MB plus 50 MB; it is kept
because the system and many apps need it.
